# Scientific evidence audit (phase A4.1)

This audit re-checked every source behind the agronomic knowledge base before any
comparison was calculated, gave every cited claim a verification status and a
required action, and put an evidence gate between the knowledge base and every
calculation. The audit record is `data/knowledge/evidence_audit.json`; the gate is
`src/evidence_audit.py`.

_Generated from the code and data on 15 November 2026 by a script; do not edit by hand._

## How the audit was done

* Every one of the thirteen registered sources was requested again on 15 November 2026. Each answered with HTTP status 200 at the address in the registry, or through the DOI resolver for the two journal articles.
* Title, authors, journal, volume, issue, pages and year of the two journal articles were compared again with their Crossref records.
* Both journal abstracts were read again from Europe PMC and every claim resting on them was compared with the abstract text.
* For every value that phase A4 calculates with, the source page was downloaded again and the printed figures were compared with the knowledge base. Claim groups re-checked in this way are listed under rechecked_claims.
* Claims that A4 does not use in any calculation (for example crop coefficients, plant heights, salinity ratings and growth-stage lengths) were not read again. Their status rests on the A3 reading of 14 November 2026 and is shown as such.
* Statuses are derived by rule from how much of the source was read and the confidence the A3 reading assigned. The exceptions, each with its reason, are listed under claim_overrides.

## The method source added in A4

The scenario engine needs definitions of a wet day, a dry spell, a heavy-rain day and a frost day. They
come from the ETCCDI climate-index definitions, recorded in the audit file as a method source (not an
agronomic source, so the thirteen-source registry is unchanged):

* Expert Team on Climate Change Detection and Indices (ETCCDI). Climate Change Indices: Definitions of the 27 core indices. https://etccdi.pacificclimate.org/list_27_indices.shtml (undated web page, accessed 15 November 2026)
  * FD: annual count of days when the daily minimum temperature is below 0 °C
  * CDD: maximum number of consecutive days with daily precipitation below 1 mm
  * R10mm and R20mm: count of days with precipitation of at least 10 mm and 20 mm
  * Rx1day: maximum 1-day precipitation
  * SDII: mean precipitation on wet days, a wet day being one with at least 1 mm

Limitations: The indices are defined for station records over a year or a month. CropShift applies the same definitions within a rainy-season window and to a reanalysis grid-cell average, which is an adaptation of their intended use. A frost day by this definition is a statement about air temperature at 2 m. It does not establish crop damage, and frost can form on the ground when the air above stays above 0 °C.

## The evidence gate

| Use requested | Admitted | Refused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numeric input (drives a result) | VERIFIED_DIRECT; VERIFIED_CONDITIONAL with its conditions attached; only claims re-checked in this audit | ABSTRACT_ONLY, UNVERIFIED, CONTRADICTED, unregistered sources, values not re-checked, stage-specific values when stage dates are unknown, a requirement used for a crop it does not belong to |
| Background (explains a result) | VERIFIED_DIRECT, VERIFIED_CONDITIONAL, ABSTRACT_ONLY (with an 'abstract only' condition) | UNVERIFIED, CONTRADICTED |

A refused value makes its indicator unavailable; nothing is substituted. The result contracts enforce
the same rules a second time: an indicator built on conditional evidence cannot be marked supported,
a conditional indicator must display its conditions, and abstract-only evidence cannot be recorded as
a numeric input. The tests in `tests/test_evidence_audit.py` and `tests/test_comparison_engine.py` try
each of these routes and confirm that each is closed.
